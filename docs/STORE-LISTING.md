## English

**Name**: FUCustomer (奧客退散)

**Subtitle (≤30 chars)**: Survive the worst customers

**Short description**:
A first-person convenience-store sim where the customer is always right — until they aren't. Ring up sales, restock shelves, brew coffee and mop floors while dealing with line-cutters, ragers, livestreamers and litterers. When all else fails: pick them up and throw them out the door.

**Full description**:
Welcome to the corner store. The customer is king — but karens get kicked out.

🧑‍💼 Career mode — 6 chapters, 30 shifts, 30 ranks
Work your way from a part-time trainee on the day shift to the legendary "Ultimate Worker". Each rank grants skill points across three trees (Service, Stamina, Crisis), and your wages buy better shoes, mops and coffee machines.

🛒 Actually run the store
Check out customers, haul stock from the back room, brew coffee, microwave bento boxes and mop up spills. Be too slow and customers storm out; let your rating drop and business dries up.

😤 A rogues' gallery of terrible customers
Line-cutters, ragers demanding refunds, livestreamers filming everything, shelf snackers, litterers and outlet hogs. Calm them down, pull the plug, bribe them with coffee, call security, spin your mop — or grab them, aim for the door and throw.

📅 Daily challenge & 20 achievements
A new shift every day with twists like frequent blackouts, streamer invasions or double crowds. Clear it once a day for rewards and keep your streak going.

👑 Boss fight & endless night shift
The King of Karens fights in five phases, each needing a different trick. Beat him to unlock the endless night shift.

👥 Up to 5-player co-op (beta)
Share a 5-character room code and run the store together.

• Single-player works fully offline, no account needed
• Keyboard & mouse, gamepad and touch controls
• No ads, no personal data collected
