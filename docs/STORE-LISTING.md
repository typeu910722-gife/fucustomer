顧客至上——但奧客，退散。

從巷口便利商店的白天班開始，做到深夜班、車站店、觀光旗艦店、跨年夜美食街，迎戰五階段的「奧客之王」，再一路轉職到電影院、火鍋店、加油站、藥局、飯店……走遍全台 21 種店。每升一級拿技能點，分配到服務、體能、危機處理三條技能樹；薪水拿去買更好的鞋、拖把、咖啡機。

### English

**Short description** (≤300 chars):
A first-person store sim where the customer is always right — until they aren't. Ring up sales, restock, brew coffee and mop floors while handling line-cutters, ragers and livestreamers. Nothing works? Pick them up and throw them out the door. Co-op for up to 5. (Traditional Chinese only)

**About this game**:

The customer is king — but karens get kicked out.

**⚠ Language: the game is currently in Traditional Chinese only.**

**Career mode — 10 chapters, 51 shifts**
Start on the day shift of a tiny corner store and work your way through night shifts, a train-station store, a tourist flagship, a New Year's Eve food court and a five-phase fight with the King of Karens — then move on to cinemas, hot-pot buffets, gas stations, pharmacies and hotels: 21 store types in all. Every rank gives skill points for the Service, Stamina and Crisis trees; spend your wages on better shoes, mops and coffee machines.

**Actually run the store**
Check out customers, haul stock from the back room, brew coffee, microwave bento boxes and mop up spills. Too slow and customers storm out; let your rating drop and business dries up.

**Every store has its signature karen**
Line-cutters, livestreamers, shelf snackers, sample hoarders, people who read a whole book in the aisle, refund ragers… 17 signature karens, each with its own counter: calm them down, bribe them with coffee, call security, spin your mop — or grab them, aim for the door and throw.

**Daily challenge & endless night shift**
A new shift every day with twists like frequent blackouts, streamer invasions or double crowds. Beat the story to unlock the endless night shift.

**Co-op for up to 5 players**
Share a 5-character room code or use Steam's "Join Game". Mobile players can join the same room.

• Single-player works fully offline, no account needed
• 22 Steam achievements, Steam Cloud saves
• No ads, no loot boxes, no pay-to-win
• Cute cartoon style, no gore
